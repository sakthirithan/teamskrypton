# Teams Krypton – Team Member Guide

Teams Krypton is an internal system to track your daily work clearly and fairly.

This guide explains **only what YOU need to know** as a Team Member.

---

## 👤 Your Access
- Login using your college email
- You can log in only after approval by Team Captain or Vice Captain

---

## 🏠 Home Page
- **Today’s Task** is always shown at the top
- Tasks stay visible until you complete them
- You only see tasks assigned to YOU

---

## ▶️ Working on a Task
1. Click **Accept Task**
   - Task moves to *In Progress*
   - Time starts automatically
2. Work on the task
3. Click **Complete Task**
   - Time ends
   - Task is marked completed

⚠️ You cannot edit time manually.

---

## ⏰ If You Miss a Deadline
- Task moves to **Pending**
- You must give a **Reason**
- Leadership will review your reason
- If approved, you can resume the task
- Or you'can resume directly

---

## 👤 My Space (Your Dashboard)
Inside **My Space**, you can see:
- In Progress tasks
- Alerts & reason requests
- Your task history
- Your performance summary

You **cannot**:
- See others’ tasks

---

## 📄 Logs & Downloads
- You can download **own task history**
- Available formats: CSV / Excel

---

## 📌 Important Rules
- No chatting inside the system
- No excuses — system records everything
- Complete tasks honestly and on time

---

✅ Focus on your work.  
The system handles the rest.
